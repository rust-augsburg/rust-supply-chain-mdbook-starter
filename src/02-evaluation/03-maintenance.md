# 2. Maintenance and governance

Look for evidence of stewardship:

- release and issue history;
- responsiveness to security reports;
- multiple active maintainers;
- documented ownership or governance;
- a changelog and compatibility policy;
- a plausible succession path.

Weak signals such as stars, downloads, and a recent commit can guide further
research, but should not decide adoption alone.

> [!CAUTION]
Good governance does not remove the need to inspect what the crate executes.
